# Meteo Rwanda forecast time scales

- Nowcasting: 1 minute to about 2 hours
- Short-range: 3 hours to 3 days
- Medium-range: 3 days to under 10 days
- Extended-range: 10 to 30 days, usually as a departure from climate
- Monthly outlook: that month versus climate
- Seasonal forecast: about 90 days versus climate

The days list on the Teganyamuhinzi Forecasts page is the OpenWeatherMap short-range forecast. It is not a Meteo Rwanda bulletin. If a longer OpenWeather subscription is added, more days can appear in that list.
