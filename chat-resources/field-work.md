# Spray, weeding and field work

Do not spray in strong wind (about 30 km/h or more) or when rain is close — product washes off or drifts.

Dry or trace rain, and a low chance of rain: better window for spraying, top-dressing or walking fields.

Light rain: weeding and planting can continue; finish spraying early so it can dry.

Moderate or heavy rain: avoid spraying; watch erosion on slopes; check drainage. After heavy rain, wait for the surface to firm.

Only mention soil moisture when there is a field check or a validated estimate. Use the Soil suitability page for crop suitability maps. Do not guess soil wetness from the sky forecast alone.
