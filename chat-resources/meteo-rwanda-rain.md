# Rain labels (millimetres from OpenWeatherMap)

Live rain millimetres, chance, temperature and wind come from OpenWeatherMap via the Teganyamuhinzi backend. Do not invent millimetres. Do not say Meteo Rwanda supplied the numbers.

- 0 mm: No rain / Ntamvura
- above 0 mm and under 1 mm: Trace / Akayira
- 1 mm to under 5 mm: Light rain / Imvura nke
- 5 mm to under 20 mm: Moderate rain / Imvura iringaniye
- 20 mm to 50 mm: Heavy rain / Imvura nyinshi
- above 50 mm: Very heavy rain / Imvura nyinshi cyane

Cloud cover: under 30% few clouds; 30% to under 60% partly cloudy; 60%+ mostly cloudy.

When writing a farmer sentence, prefer these words. You may also show the millimetres from the live forecast if they are present.
