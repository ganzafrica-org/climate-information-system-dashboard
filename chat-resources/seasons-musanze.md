# Crop seasons in Musanze, Rwanda

Season A (Umuhindo / long rains): September to February. Main season. Maize, beans, Irish potatoes in the volcanic highlands.

Season B (Itumba / short rains): March to June. Second season. Beans, vegetables, potato follow-on.

Season C: July to August. Drier. Marshland crops, irrigation, land preparation.

Musanze sectors: Busogo, Cyuve, Gacaca, Gashaki, Gataraga, Kimonyi, Kinigi, Muhoza, Muko, Musanze, Nkotsi, Nyange, Remera, Rwaza, Shingiro.

Planting dates still depend on the local sector forecast and soil moisture in the field. This is decision support for agronomists, not an order to plant on a given day.
